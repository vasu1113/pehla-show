THE INHERITANCE
Episode 1

INT. LIVING ROOM — DAY

NARRATOR
The Sharma family had lived in the city of Nagpur for four generations. There
were five of them in the house. There was Vinod, who was the father, and he
worked at the bank, which he had done for twenty-two years. There was Sunita,
who was his wife, and she had been a teacher before she was married and was not
one now. There was their elder son Anil, who was studying commerce, and their
younger daughter Priya, who was studying science, and Vinod's brother Deepak,
who had come to stay after his business did not work out and had stayed for a
long time after that.

VINOD
Good morning, everyone. It is a normal morning in our house.

SUNITA
Yes, it is a normal morning. I have made breakfast, as I do every morning,
because that is what I do.

ANIL
Good morning, Father. Good morning, Mother. Good morning, Uncle. Good morning,
Priya.

PRIYA
Good morning, Anil. Good morning, everyone.

DEEPAK
Good morning. As you all know, I have been living here since my business did not
work out three years ago.

VINOD
Yes, we all know that.

NARRATOR
The house itself was built in 1974. It had four rooms and a courtyard at the
back where a neem tree grew. The neem tree had been planted by Vinod's father in
1981. The courtyard was swept every morning by Sunita, unless she was unwell, in
which case it was swept by Priya. In the monsoon the courtyard would flood a
little at the eastern corner, which was a known problem that everyone in the
family was aware of and had been aware of for some time.

SUNITA
The tea is ready.

VINOD
Thank you. Tea is a beverage that we drink in the morning.

ANIL
I will now go to my college.

PRIYA
I will also go to my college.

NARRATOR
And so the day began, as days do, in the house where the Sharma family lived,
in the city of Nagpur, where they had lived for four generations.

END OF EPISODE 1
